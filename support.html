Support

How does filtering work?

The app opens supported sites in an in-app view and, on your device, hides recommendation feeds and short-video sections. It can also end a feed after the number of items you set in settings. Nothing is sent to us.

Why do I log in inside the app?

The app shows the real site in its own web view, which keeps its session separate from your phone's browser and other apps. Your login goes directly to the site, and we never see or store your credentials. If you log out or clear the app's data, you will need to log in again.

Something is not hidden, or a page looks broken

Sites change their layout often. Please email us with the site, the page, and what you saw; updated rules are delivered with app updates.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Google Play: Play Store, profile icon, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, No line, Cancel. App Store: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, No line, Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal or before a free trial ends. See the Terms.

How do I restore purchases?

Use "Restore purchases" on the paywall or in settings while signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought with. If it does not work, contact us with your order ID.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple; request one through your store's order history or support page.
