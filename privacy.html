Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-16. Last updated: 2026-11-20.

This policy explains how the "No line" app (Android app ID com.noline.app, "the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is provided by Soulten, a sole proprietorship registered in the Republic of Kazakhstan. In short: we operate no servers that receive your personal data, and we do not run analytics or advertising in the app.

1. Information we collect

On our servers: none. The app has no account system and no backend of our own that stores your personal information.

Stored locally on your device. The app saves your settings on your device only: whether you chose to use it for yourself or for a child, which sites are enabled, your feed-length setting, a YouTube subscriptions-only option, the list of apps selected in child mode, and whether onboarding is complete. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases. Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle your payment details; we never receive your card number. We use RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history (products, dates, status, store receipts or tokens), and basic technical information such as device type, OS version, and IP address, in order to verify entitlements. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Third-party websites. The app displays sites such as instagram.com and youtube.com inside a web view. These pages load directly from those companies and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Your login session and cookies are kept by the web view on your device, as in a browser. We do not read, copy, transmit, or store your passwords, credentials, messages, or browsing activity. The app only applies display rules (hiding certain page elements) on your device.

Camera, microphone and photos. The app uses the camera, the microphone or your photos and files only when you choose to send media inside one of these sites, for example a voice message, a photo or a video in Instagram Direct. The app first asks for your permission (Android shows a system prompt; you can say no and keep using everything else). What you record or pick goes directly from your device to that site, as it would in a browser. We do not record, view, copy, upload to our own servers, or keep any of it; a photo or video taken with the camera for an upload is held only in the app's temporary cache on your device. You can turn camera and microphone access off at any time in your device settings.

Screen time (usage access, Android, optional)

If you grant Android "Usage access", the app reads how much time you spent in social apps (for example Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook) during the previous days, and counts time spent inside No line. It uses this only to show you a before/after comparison and a reminder near the end of your trial. These numbers are processed and stored only on your device; they are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can revoke usage access at any time in Android Settings, and uninstalling the app deletes the stored numbers.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your device (for example before your free trial ends). No notification data is sent to a server.

2. Crash reporting and analytics [EDIT HERE WHEN ADDED]

The app currently does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. If we add such a service in the future, this section will be updated to name the provider and the data it receives (for example, crash logs and device model), and the effective date above will change.

3. How we use information

Local settings are used to run the app. Purchase information is used to provide and verify paid features and to restore purchases. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. "Child mode" is a tool for parents and guardians to set up the app on a device used by a child; it should be configured by an adult, and purchases must be made by an adult account holder. Because we collect no personal information on our servers, we hold no child data. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact soulten.app@gmail.com and we will delete it. This approach is intended to be consistent with COPPA (US) and GDPR Article 8 (EU/UK).

5. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are retained as needed to provide subscription services and meet legal obligations. To request deletion of data associated with your anonymous app user ID, email soulten.app@gmail.com with your app user ID (if the app shows it) or your store order ID; we will forward the request to RevenueCat and confirm. Purchase data held by Google or Apple is managed under their own policies.

6. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents may also request to know what is collected and to opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Contact soulten.app@gmail.com to make a request. Where GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing purchased features) and legitimate interests (verifying entitlements and preventing fraud).

7. Security and international transfers

Local settings stay in your device's app storage. RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries using appropriate safeguards.

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
